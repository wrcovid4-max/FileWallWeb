    The small print

    Things worth stating plainly.

    1. FileWall is available for Android and is free to download.
    2. The iPhone, Apple Watch, Vision, XR, CarPlay, Android Auto and web versions are in development and not yet available — the buttons for those platforms are placeholders.
    3. FileWall has not been independently audited or certified. The design is documented in full on this site and the source is public.
    4. Encryption keys are generated in the Android Keystore and are hardware-backed on most, but not all, devices.
    5. Google Drive backup requires a Google Cloud OAuth client that you create yourself, and an internet connection.
    6. If you lose a backup passphrase, that archive cannot be recovered by anyone, including us.
    7. Android, Google Play, the Google Play logo, Google Drive, Wear OS, Android Auto and Android XR are trademarks of Google LLC.
