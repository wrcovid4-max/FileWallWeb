    Privacy Policy

    WR COVID TM (“we,” “our,” or “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how FileWall collects, uses, and protects your information.

    Effective date: August 3, 2026 Developer: WR COVID TM Contact: wr.covid.4@gmail.com

    1. Information We Collect 2. Files 3. Information We Do Not Collect 4. How We Use Information 5. Google Sign-In 6. Data Storage 7. Security 8. Data Retention 9. Children's Privacy 10. International Users 11. Changes to This Privacy Policy 12. Contact

    1. Information We Collect

    Depending on how you use FileWall, we may collect:

    • Your Google Account information if you choose to sign in using Google.
    • Basic account identifiers required for authentication.
    • App settings and preferences stored on your device.

    2. Files

    FileWall stores your files locally on your device.

    We do not claim ownership of your files.

    3. Information We Do Not Collect

    At the time of this Policy, FileWall does not:

    • Collect analytics.
    • Collect crash reports.
    • Display advertisements.
    • Sell personal information.
    • Use your data for advertising.
    • Use your files to train artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

    4. How We Use Information

    Information is used only to:

    • Authenticate your optional Google Sign-In.
    • Operate the App.
    • Maintain your preferences.
    • Improve security and functionality.

    5. Google Sign-In

    If you choose to use Google Sign-In, authentication is provided through Google's secure services.

    Your use of Google Sign-In is also subject to Google's applicable policies and terms.

    6. Data Storage

    Files managed by FileWall are stored locally on your device unless you intentionally move or share them using another service.

    7. Security

    We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information processed by the App. However, no electronic storage or transmission method is completely secure.

    8. Data Retention

    Information remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall FileWall.

    Google authentication information is retained only as necessary to maintain your signed-in session.

    9. Children's Privacy

    FileWall is intended for users who are 16 years of age or older.

    We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 16.

    10. International Users

    By using FileWall, you acknowledge that information may be processed in accordance with applicable laws where required for services you choose to use.

    11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

    We may revise this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updated versions will become effective upon publication.
