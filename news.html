    The Wear OS companion is here

    Browse your unlocked files from the watch, and open photos right there. Video and documents show a preview plus an Open on phone button that sends a notification rather than opening anything remotely.

    Features

    Video plays straight from the encrypted file

    Playback now streams from the ciphertext, so no temporary decrypted copy is ever written to disk. Seeking decrypts only the region you jump to, which is what makes scrubbing a long video practical in constant memory.

    May 18, 2026

    Screenshots are blocked by default

    The vault sets the platform's secure-window flag, which also keeps it out of screen recordings and the app-switcher preview. It can be turned on if you genuinely need to capture the screen.

    Daily Wi-Fi-only backups to your own Drive

    Backups can now run on a schedule instead of only on demand. The archive is encrypted with your passphrase before it is uploaded, and restores are verified before anything is written.

    Security

    Escalating lockout on the hidden archive

    Five wrong passcode attempts now start a lockout that grows each time it happens, so guessing a short code costs real time rather than none.

    Mar 11, 2026

    Engineering

    Why we chose CTR and a separate MAC over GCM

    GCM will not release verified plaintext until it has buffered the whole message — for a video that means holding the entire file in RAM. This is the reasoning behind the trailing HMAC instead.

    Feb 24, 2026
