    FileWall on iPhone.

    The same private vault, keyed to the Secure Enclave. Files are encrypted on the device and unlocked with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode — with a hidden vault behind a second passcode.

    The same vault, on iPhone.

    FileWall keeps your files encrypted on the device and unlocks with the hardware you already trust. No account, no server, nothing to sign up for.

    • Unlocks with your face. Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode open the vault; the keys are kept in the Secure Enclave.
    • A hidden vault inside the vault. A second space behind its own passcode, locked by default.
    • Nothing leaves without you. Screenshots are off by default; export a single file, or an encrypted .fwvault archive, when you choose.
